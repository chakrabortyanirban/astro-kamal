Core Website ComponentsAn astrologer's website should ideally be built on a deep, dark color palette (deep blues, purples, or blacks with gold accents) to invoke cosmic energy.Hero Section: A bold headline declaring the astrologer's specialty, a professional portrait, and a clear call-to-action button linking directly to the contact/booking form.About the Astrologer: A brief biography detailing their years of experience, lineage or training, certifications, and philosophy.Services Grid: Clear description blocks for offerings like Birth Chart Reading, Relationship Compatibility (Synastry), Career Guidance, or Gemstone Consultation.Testimonials: Text or video reviews from clients sharing how the readings guided their lives.The Contact / Consultation Form: The highly operational section where users submit their highly specific personal and birth parameters.

Key Visuals & Images to IncludeInstead of using generic clip art, focus on clean, high-contrast, modern mystical assets.
Cosmic Elements: High-resolution backgrounds of starry skies, nebula dust, and high-quality gold vector illustrations of constellations or the 12 zodiac symbols.Astrological Charts: Stylized graphic representations of the Astro Wheel, Western birth charts, or Vedic Kundali grids.Tools of the Trade: Professional photos showing actual tools used, such as authentic crystals, tarot cards, or ancient text manuscripts.


Advanced Contact Form & Multifile Upload SpecificationsAstrology requires highly specific entry data to eliminate guesswork. Implement the following fields and file upload features:1. Standard FieldsFull Name: For personalized analysis.Email & Phone Number: For delivering reports or scheduling calls.Date of Birth: Calibrated calendar selector (DD/MM/YYYY format).Exact Time of Birth: Hour, minute, and AM/PM toggle. Even a few minutes' difference completely alters the rising sign and house placements.Place of Birth: City, State/Region, and Country to pull precise latitude and longitude variables.2. Multifile Upload ModuleClients often prefer uploading existing documentation. Your form needs a drag-and-drop multifile upload field with strict validation rules.What clients can upload:Scanned photos of their palm lines (for palmistry cross-referencing).Pictures of a previously drawn physical Kundali or birth chart.Screenshots of birth certificates (to confirm exact registration time).Technical Constraints to Program:Allowed formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, and .pdf only.Max file count: Limit to 3 files per submission to prevent server spam.Max file size: Restrict to 5MB per file to keep loading times efficient.Storage security: Ensure files upload directly into a secure directory (like AWS S3 or Google Cloud Storage) and encrypt the storage path to protect sensitive personal identity data.
